Getting started · Login · Your details 6

New here? Start with the details you need and how to log in.

Can I get a reading without knowing my birth time? ↗

Choose “Birth time unknown” on the input screen if you don’t know it. Leaving it unknown helps distinguish what the reading can cover, rather than entering a made-up time as fact.

What should I prepare before a consultation? ↗

Prepare your name or preferred name, birth date, birth time, gender, and whether the date is solar or lunar. For a lunar date, also check whether it falls in a leap month.

Do I need to log in to read the introduction or FAQ? ↗

You can read the public MY guide, the Fortune Shop introduction, and FAQ without logging in. Log in to see account-linked information such as your personal profile, orders, and purchased results.

Do I need my partner’s birth date for compatibility? ↗

Couple compatibility, marriage compatibility, and relationship-signal consultations need your partner’s details, including their birth date. Not knowing their birth time is different from not knowing their birth date.

Can I view the same consultation on my computer and phone? ↗

You can use it in a browser on a computer or mobile device. Introductions and FAQs adapt to the screen width, and account-based records need to be viewed with the same login account.

What can I try for free first? ↗

“What luck is coming my way today?” is free. Before choosing a paid consultation, you can check today’s flow and get familiar with the screen layout and how to read it.

Payment · Refunds 6

Check what to review before paying, how to find orders, and the cancellation and refund process.

What can I see before paying? ↗

You can check the consultation introduction, price, preview, and paid contents first. For consultations where your inputs determine the comparison scope, such as choosing a wedding date, also check that you entered the right candidates.

Is this a subscription or pay-per-consultation? ↗

The paid consultations currently listed are purchased individually by product. Prices vary by service, so don’t assume one product’s price applies to other consultations.

Do I need to pay again for a reading with the same details? ↗

If you already bought the consultation, first open the orders or saved results for the same account. It’s best to avoid repeatedly buying a new consultation without checking your existing purchase.

How are refund requests reviewed? ↗

Refunds are reviewed based on whether content generation or delivery has started, service failures, differences between what was displayed and what was provided, and similar factors. Simply opening a screen does not determine every case.

Where do I check payment methods and card details? ↗

The current guidance is based on card payments. Check available methods and the approved amount in the actual payment window, and enter payment information on the payment provider’s screen.

I paid, but the result won’t open. What should I check? ↗

First check that you’re logged in with the account you used to pay and check the order status. There may be a problem between payment approval and result display, so don’t pay again right away.

Safety, movement paths, ease of use, and the home’s actual conditions come first. Feng shui interpretation is not a tool for verifying placement effects or confirming the safety of structural changes.
