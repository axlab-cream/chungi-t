Basic principles

Paid products at Fortune Shop are services in which digital content is generated or viewing begins after the order.

Criteria for cancellation eligibility

  • You may request cancellation through support before report generation or content viewing starts after payment.
  • If a service outage prevents delivery of paid content, we will verify it and arrange cancellation or redelivery.
  • If the advertised product and actual content clearly differ, refunds are guided through support verification.

Refund restrictions

  • Refunds may be restricted if you have already viewed a report generated from incorrect information you entered.
  • After generation or delivery of personalized digital content has started, withdrawal may be limited within the range the relevant law allows.
  • Change-of-mind refunds are reviewed according to whether content delivery began and payment-provider processing criteria.

How to handle it

Send the payment date, payer details, and reason with your refund request. Approved refunds use the original payment method; timing may vary with the card issuer or payment processor.
