FIVE MENTORS

Different advice from five mentors

Even with the same quitting concern, Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water have different criteria. Rather than pushing one answer, choose the attitude needed now.

Wood TeacherLook at growth and direction. Ask whether the current company bends your branches or prepares the next season.
Fire mentorLooks at passion and exhaustion, separating whether anger is posing as resolve or whether the stage you truly want has opened up.
Earth mentorIt looks at the base of daily life. Rather than a structure for holding on after you leave, it advises setting an order of sorting that does not collapse first.
Master GeumLook at contracts and boundaries. Rather than ending things on emotion alone, smooth the sharp edges around words, documents, handover, and reputation.
Water mentorLook at recovery and the next flow. When no answer is visible yet, do not flee under the pretense of resting; quietly listen again for direction.
