My partner and I keep fighting about the same thing. Where should I look?

+

Couple compatibility covers communication styles, recurring conflicts, and the practical conditions of your relationship. You enter both people’s details and organize your current relationship stage and concerns.

Choose one topic you often fight about and write down the situation and each person’s reactions. The result is a reference for finding differences to check, not a tool for deciding who is wrong. Please also remember that real conversation and agreement are central to resolving conflict.

What do I enter for couple compatibility?

+

You need your chart details and your partner’s birth date, gender, solar/lunar date setting, and other requested details. Please enter their birth time if you know it; otherwise choose “Birth time unknown.”

Q

Does couple compatibility decide whether we should get married?

+

Couple compatibility takes a broad look at attraction, communication, arguments, and how you handle your relationship. It doesn’t tell you the right answer about marriage. If you want to focus on the practical side of married life, compare the Marriage compatibility sections.

Before marriage, there’s a lot to agree on directly, such as housing, money management, plans for children, and family relationships. Rather than deciding from a compatibility summary alone, it’s better to use it to organize questions you both need to answer in real life.

Should we break up if the result says we’re incompatible?

+

You don’t need to decide on a breakup from an interpretation alone. An explanation that your tendencies differ is not the same as a judgment that you cannot maintain the relationship.

Please check the full text and conditions for when differences arise, and compare them with actual conflicts. You can use it as a reference to separate habits you can agree on from nonnegotiable boundaries, but safety and respect in the relationship must be judged separately from fortune readings.

Q

How does marriage compatibility differ from wedding-date selection?

+

Marriage compatibility looks at your relationship and married life; wedding-date selection compares the candidate ceremony or marriage-registration dates you enter. Issues between people and choosing a schedule are different questions.

If you’re unsure about living together, check Marriage compatibility. If you’ve decided to marry and want to compare dates, check Wedding-date selection. A favorable date in a reading does not remove relationship difficulties or guarantee how married life will turn out.

Can marriage compatibility cover money management and daily habits?

Can I compare candidate wedding dates?

+

Wedding-date selection compares the candidate dates you enter. It currently accepts at least one and at most three candidates, and looks at the conditions for each.

What if I don’t know the partner details for wedding-date selection?

+

Wedding-date selection asks for partner details differently from Couple compatibility. The main comparison is between the candidate dates. Whether you provide your partner’s details can change how much of the reading considers both of you together.
