Q

Can I get a reading without knowing my birth time?

+

Choose “Birth time unknown” on the input screen if you don’t know it. Leaving it unknown helps distinguish what the reading can cover, rather than entering a made-up time as fact.

Read the parts based on your birth date separately from those that depend on the hour pillar. You can update your profile if you learn the exact time later, but results already created won’t automatically update to match those details.

Q

What should I prepare before a consultation?

+

Prepare your name or preferred name, birth date, birth time, gender, and whether the date is solar or lunar. For a lunar date, also check whether it falls in a leap month.

Extra details vary by service. Prepare what the screen asks for, such as your partner’s details for compatibility, candidate dates for choosing a wedding date, or the company, role, and working conditions for a job choice. Please don’t put passwords, card numbers, or other unrelated information in the concern field.

Q

Do I need to log in to read the introduction or FAQ?

+

You can read the public MY guide, the Fortune Shop introduction, and FAQ without logging in. Log in to see account-linked information such as your personal profile, orders, and purchased results.

It’s fine to read the guides before choosing a consultation. If you reach a login screen, please check the address and use the social account you used before. If you sign in with a different account, previous purchases won’t appear as purchases on that account.

Q

Do I need my partner’s birth date for compatibility?

+

Couple compatibility, marriage compatibility, and relationship-signal consultations need your partner’s details, including their birth date. Not knowing their birth time is different from not knowing their birth date.

If you don’t know the time, choose “Birth time unknown.” Don’t guess the birth date. Without your partner’s details, you can first explore your own relationship tendencies through this year’s love reading or Four Pillars Reading, using your own details.

Q

Can I view the same consultation on my computer and phone?

+

You can use it in a browser on a computer or mobile device. Introductions and FAQs adapt to the screen width, and account-based records need to be viewed with the same login account.

If the text on your phone is small, try browser zoom or your device’s text-size setting. A computer may be more convenient for printing, saving PDFs, or comparing a long table of contents. Changing devices alone won’t transfer purchased results to a new account.

Q

What can I try for free first?

+

“What luck is coming my way today?” is free. Before choosing a paid consultation, you can check today’s flow and get familiar with the screen layout and how to read it.

For a paid consultation, check the free preview and paid scope on its product page first. The preview isn’t the full report, so it’s a good idea to check whether the topic you want is in the paid contents before paying.
