Q

What should I write in the Relationship signals concern field?

+

Write specific events you’ve confirmed instead of guessing hidden feelings. Start with actual observations, such as the relationship stage, frequently changed plans, changes in contact style, or conversations you’ve already had.

For example, please distinguish “our plans were postponed twice recently and I haven’t asked why yet” from the conclusion “they dislike me.” You don’t need to enter excessive personal information or full transcripts of other people’s private conversations.

How should I talk to my partner after reading the result?

+

Don’t present the result as a verdict; turn it into questions you can check in real life. Separating observed changes, your feelings, and standards you want to agree on helps focus the discussion.

Rather than conclude their feelings have cooled because replies are late, ask whether you can agree on a comfortable way to stay in touch now that contact times have changed. Please prioritize their answers and actual actions over fortune-reading sentences.

Q

My relationship is frightening or dangerous. Can fortune readings solve it?

+

Do not try to resolve safety issues such as threats, coercion, or violence through a fortune reading. If you are in immediate danger, prioritize getting to safety and seeking appropriate public support.

Relationship signals does not assess how dangerous someone is or guarantee your safety. A reading that describes a chart connection is no reason to endure uncomfortable contact or be forced to stay in a relationship. Please keep reference readings separate from real safety issues.
