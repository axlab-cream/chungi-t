What questions should I ask after a comprehensive Four Pillars reading?

+

Don’t stop at the conclusions that feel relevant. Write down questions you can check in real life. For money, that might be when you spend; for relationships, conversations that keep repeating; for work, the conditions that make a task difficult. Be specific.

Q

Why does New-year fortune have a separate explanation of Ipchun, the start of spring?

+

Ipchun, the start of spring, is discussed because traditional Four Pillars uses it when explaining when the year changes. This section helps you distinguish January 1 on the calendar from the point used in traditional Four Pillars interpretation.

When reading explanations about before and after Ipchun, please distinguish actual schedule dates from the interpretation’s reference point. Administrative or legal standards, such as contract effectiveness or age, do not change according to a fortune reading’s year transition.
