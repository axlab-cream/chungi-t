Q

What can I see before paying?

+

You can check the consultation introduction, price, preview, and paid contents first. For consultations where your inputs determine the comparison scope, such as choosing a wedding date, also check that you entered the right candidates.

A few explanations in the preview don’t replace the full result. Please decide whether to pay after checking that your question is in the contents, your required details are accurate, and you understand the scope and refund policy.

Q

Is this a subscription or pay-per-consultation?

+

The paid consultations currently listed are purchased individually by product. Prices vary by service, so don’t assume one product’s price applies to other consultations.

Check the final amount on the selected consultation and order screen. Service offerings may change, so rely on the current page rather than an old screenshot. If the amount looks different, check with customer support before paying.

Q

Do I need to pay again for a reading with the same details?

+

If you already bought the consultation, first open the orders or saved results for the same account. It’s best to avoid repeatedly buying a new consultation without checking your existing purchase.

Changing inputs that distinguish results, such as a birth date, partner details, or candidate dates, may be treated as a different consultation. If the payment screen appears despite an existing purchase, check the order and login account and contact customer support.

Q

How are refund requests reviewed?

+

Refunds are reviewed based on whether content generation or delivery has started, service failures, differences between what was displayed and what was provided, and similar factors. Simply opening a screen does not determine every case.

Cancellation may be restricted after generation or delivery of personalized content begins. If a failure prevented you from receiving the content or the content is clearly different, please contact us with your order and a description of what happened. Specific handling follows the refund and cancellation policy and verification process.

Q

Where do I check payment methods and card details?

+

The current guidance is based on card payments. Check available methods and the approved amount in the actual payment window, and enter payment information on the payment provider’s screen.

Please don’t send your full card number, security code, or password in the concern field or a general support inquiry. For payment inquiries, the payment date, product name, amount, order identifiers, and error details help us check.

Q

I paid, but the result won’t open. What should I check?

+

First check that you’re logged in with the account you used to pay and check the order status. There may be a problem between payment approval and result display, so don’t pay again right away.

Try reopening the consultation from your orders. If it still won’t open, record when it happened and the error message. Sending the payment date, product name, and amount to customer support helps check both payment and content-delivery status.
