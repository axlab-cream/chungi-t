Q

Where can I view a purchased result again?

+

Look for saved readings and consultation records in your saved results. The Destiny Record menu is for personal chart information such as your natal chart, symbolic stars, and major luck cycles; it serves a different purpose.

If an earlier result isn’t visible, please first check that you’re using the same social login account. Retention and deletion follow the privacy policy; this does not guarantee indefinite storage. If you keep important content separately, please store it safely on your own device.

Q

Can someone else see my result if I send them the link?

+

Personal results are normally viewed through your own account. Don’t assume another person can open a result just because you send the link. Service introductions and FAQs can be shared through public links.

Before sharing result content, please check the birth dates, names, partner details, and concerns it contains. Screenshots or files may include someone else’s personal information, so share only what is needed and consider their consent.

Q

My reading stopped midway. What should I check?

+

Check your network connection and the screen’s error or retry guidance first. Saved states can vary by service, so distinguish completed sections from those not yet generated.

Reopen your saved results or the existing result with the same account. If it stops again, tell customer support which product and section are affected. Don’t delete your account or clear browser storage first in an attempt to fix the error.

Q

Can I save a result as a PDF or print it?

+

You can save a PDF or print through your browser’s print feature. Check what will actually be saved against the content displayed on the result screen and the browser’s print preview.

Don’t assume collapsed sections or content not yet generated will be included automatically. Check the content you need on screen, then review the preview. Saved files contain personal information, so it’s best not to leave them on shared computers or in public folders.

Q

If I correct my chart details, will old results change too?

+

Treat profile updates separately from results already created. Past results were based on the details entered at the time, so don’t assume they automatically convert to a reading of your latest profile.

If you entered the wrong birth date or solar/lunar setting, first correct the details and distinguish the existing result from the new inputs. How a purchase made with incorrect inputs is handled, and whether a refund is possible, must be checked separately against the order status and policy.
