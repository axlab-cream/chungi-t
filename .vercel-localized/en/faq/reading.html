Q

Does it tell me exactly which days are good or bad?

+

Fortune Shop explains conditions found in dates and charts for reference and does not guarantee success or failure on a particular day. Wedding-date selection also compares your entered candidates using the same criteria.

Please choose dates alongside practical considerations such as venue availability, family travel, costs, and health. Favorable and cautionary conditions can serve as a checklist, but they are not predictions that those events will definitely happen.

Q

Do you predict exam results, illness, divorce, or investment returns?

+

Fortune readings cannot establish important facts such as passing an exam, a disease diagnosis, a divorce outcome, or investment returns. The content is for reference and does not replace professional medical, legal, or investment judgment.

Please judge exams by actual preparation and official results, health by medical care, and contracts or disputes by relevant professional review. Rather than repeatedly seeking a conclusion out of anxiety, first gather information you can verify in real life.

Q

What information does a reading use?

+

We look at the chart calculated from your entered birth date and time together with the situation for each consultation. Even with the same chart, the focus changes depending on the question and candidates you enter.

When reading a result, please distinguish the calculation basis, entered facts, and interpretations or suggestions. A chart interpretation does not scientifically prove real events or guarantee the future. Input errors can also change the premises of related interpretations.
