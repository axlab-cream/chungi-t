Enter your birth date, birth time, gender, and solar or lunar calendar. Once registered, you will not be asked again in the next consultation. Some consultations also ask for candidate dates, your partner’s birth date, and your current situation.

04 · Operating principles

Don’t leave your choices to fortune readings.

Personal information

Your entered birth date and generated results are linked only to your logged-in account. Another account cannot open the same result, nor can a link alone. Card information is entered on the payment provider’s screen and is not stored on the Fortune Shop server.

Retention periods, deletion requests, and account-closure handling are in the privacy policy.

View the privacy policy

Limits of the reading

This is reference content. It does not pronounce lucky or unlucky days or predict outcomes that cannot be determined, such as passing an exam, divorce, or illness. Values not produced by calculation are not used as conclusions, and unknowns are stated as unknown.

This does not replace medical, legal, or investment judgment. Check important decisions with a qualified professional.

View the terms of use

Refunds and customer support

Refunds are processed based on whether you have viewed the results. The time when a refund is actually reflected may vary with the payment provider’s processing schedule. Questions about payments, reports, and refunds are received through customer support and answered in order.
