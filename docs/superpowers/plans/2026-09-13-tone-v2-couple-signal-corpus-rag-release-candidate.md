# Tone V2 couple_signal corpus/RAG release candidate

## Goal

Version and semantically review the 12-block relationship-signal corpus without rewriting stored reports or claiming provider-output evidence.

## Boundaries

- Relationship facts are limited to user-stated status, observed actions, directly heard words, explicit agreements and confirmed schedules.
- Partner feelings, fidelity, intent, private activity and future outcomes remain unknown unless directly confirmed.
- Both charts and relationship symbols are calculated inputs for questions, not evidence of behavior or character.
- Hypothetical scenes are labeled; arbitrary durations, counts and guaranteed outcomes are prohibited.
- Contact refusal, threats, coercion, violence, stalking and unauthorized monitoring override reconciliation or dialogue suggestions.

## Slice

1. Write an executable RED contract for 2.1.0 artifacts and stored-snapshot isolation.
2. Build an explicit 12-block reviewed candidate and switch only the registry pointer for new snapshots.
3. Bind a reversible local release manifest with truthful zero-provider and zero-Production state.
4. Verify focused, related, full, typecheck, build, determinism, credential and ProjectOps gates.
5. Record closure review and sanitized knowledge.

## Non-scope

- Provider generation/evaluation, Production, customer data, Supabase/auth/payment/admin, commit, push or deployment.
